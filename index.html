01 — The project

One continuous kitchen workflow.

Home cooks often lose track of what they have, struggle to choose a meal, and repeatedly touch a device while cooking.

Our project explores a context-aware interface that connects kitchen inventory, recipe discovery, shopping needs, and guided preparation. The goal is not simply to add more features, but to make the interface adapt to the user’s changing focus—from planning to active cooking.
